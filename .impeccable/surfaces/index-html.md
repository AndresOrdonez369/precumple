## Direction contract

THESIS: The love letter as the "A Sky Full of Stars" moment of a one-night concert: every lever pull lights a stadium of wristband stars and prints one ticket of the setlist. It refuses the category default of a pink card with floating hearts and a gold casino slot.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo-to-violet night ground; a stadium bowl of LED wristband dots (pink, cyan, orange, green, violet) that waves in color; sunflower yellow as the warm primary; panda black and white as the character; metallic gold reserved by law for the jackpot only. Faces: Grandstander (fan-sign lettering), M PLUS Rounded 1c (letter body), Doto (LED dot-matrix marquee and ticket print). Paper tickets with perforated stubs in each song's band color.

STORY: She understands this was made for her, reads his love first, then what hurt him in plain first-person words, then that he understands if she leaves and still loves her; she believes the care is real; she pulls to the end, picks a gift, and answers him on WhatsApp.
